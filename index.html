Frequently asked questions

How do I create an account?

Select Sign up, enter your email address and choose a password. We send a confirmation message; follow the link in it to finish.

How do I reset my password?

Select Forgot password on the sign-in page and enter your email address. We send you a link to choose a new password.

Can I change my email address?

Yes. Open Account settings, choose Email, and confirm the change from the message we send to the new address.

How do I turn on two-factor authentication?

Open Account settings, choose Security, and follow the steps to add an authenticator app.

Where can I see my past orders?

Past orders are listed under Account, then Orders. Each entry links to its receipt.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Open Account, then Billing, and choose Cancel. Your plan stays active until the end of the billing period.

Which payment methods do you accept?

We accept major credit and debit cards. Other methods vary by region and appear at checkout.

How do I contact support?

Use the Contact form in the footer. We reply within two business days.

Do you offer refunds?

Refunds are available within 30 days of purchase. Contact support with your order number.

How do I delete my account?

Open Account, then Privacy, and choose Delete account. Deletion is permanent after a 14-day grace period.
